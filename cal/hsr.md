> HSR (Hosur Sarjapur Road) Layout is the so-called Startup Capital of India, built on
land reclaimed from Agara Lake.

Events on this page are made up of:

1. Events with addresses mentioning HSR.
2. Events no other area claims, whose coordinates fall within 12.895–12.920°N, 77.630–77.665°E.
3. And not listed in the [Unwanted Events](/cal/unwanted/) calendar.
