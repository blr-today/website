> [Main Mission](https://mainmission.in/) is a running and fitness community
  that grew out of the Indiranagar Run Club. It hosts weekly community runs in
  Cubbon Park, brand-partnered runs, and fitness sessions like HYROX training,
  pilates, spin and climbing across Bengaluru.

Events on this page are sourced from the [Main Mission events page](https://mainmission.in/#/events).
Weekly runs are usually published only a few days ahead.
