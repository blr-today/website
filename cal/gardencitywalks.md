> [Garden City Walks](https://gardencitywalks.com/) runs slow, two-hour nature
  and heritage walks through Bangalore's neighbourhoods, led by Radha
  Rangarajan and Suchi Govindarajan. Each walk is for a small group of about
  20 people, with stories of the city's trees, birds and history.

Events on this page are the upcoming walks listed on the Garden City Walks
website. Book through each walk's Google Form.
