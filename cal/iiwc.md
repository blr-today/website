> The [Indian Institute of World Culture](https://iiwc.in/) is a cultural
  institution in Basavanagudi, founded in 1945. It hosts talks, music and dance
  recitals, poetry readings, exhibitions and learning programmes, and runs a
  public library and reading room.

Events on this page are sourced from the [IIWC events calendar](https://iiwc.in/events/).
