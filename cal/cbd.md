> The Central Bangalore District is everything within a short auto ride of
MG Road, from Ulsoor to Cubbon Park and up to Cox Town.

Events on this page are made up of:

1. Events with addresses mentioning MG Road, Ashok Nagar, Church Street, Residency Road, Lavelle Road, Museum Road, St Mark's Road, Cubbon Park, Kasturba Road, Shivaji Nagar, Shanthala Nagar, Shanti Nagar, Jayamahal, Vasanth Nagar, Ulsoor/Halasuru, Cox Town, Cooke Town, Frazer Town or Richards Town.
2. Events no other area claims, whose coordinates fall within 12.960–13.012°N, 77.575–77.625°E.
3. And not listed in the [Unwanted Events](/cal/unwanted/) calendar.
