> The [Indian Music Experience](https://indianmusicexperience.org/) in JP Nagar is an
  interactive music museum, from Bollywood legends and folk traditions to classical
  maestros and indie bands.

## Visiting the museum

The museum is on Brigade Millennium Avenue in JP Nagar 7th Phase, Bengaluru 560078,
about 2 km from Yelachenahalli Metro station. Inside are a Sound Garden of playable
musical sculptures, galleries with instruments, touchscreens and mini-theatres, a
learning centre with classes and workshops, and a cafe.

- **Open:** Tuesday to Sunday, closed on Mondays.
- **Timings:** 10 am to 6 pm Tuesday to Friday, and 11 am to 7 pm on weekends.
- **Tickets:** ₹200 for adults and ₹150 for children and senior citizens, booked on BookMyShow.
