> [Ranga Shankara](https://rangashankara.org/) is a theatre in JP Nagar,
  opened in 2004 in memory of Shankar Nag. Its "a play a day" programme
  stages plays in Kannada, English, Hindi and other languages almost every
  evening except Mondays, along with festivals and workshops.

Events on this page are sourced from the [upcoming shows](https://rangashankara.org/book-tickets-2/)
page. Tickets are sold on BookMyShow.
