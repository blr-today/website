> [Offlyn](https://offlyn.life/) is an app where people host small, ticketed
  plans in their city: lectures in bars, film screenings, supper clubs, run
  clubs, craft sessions and house parties.

Events on this page are the Bengaluru experiences listed on Offlyn. Many of
them are also listed on other platforms, and are merged with those listings.
