> [Alliance Française de Bangalore](https://bangalore.afindia.org/) is the
  French cultural centre on Thimmaiah Road, Vasanth Nagar. Besides French
  classes, it hosts film screenings, concerts, exhibitions, talks and festivals.

Events on this page are sourced from the [Alliance Française events calendar](https://bangalore.afindia.org/upcoming-events-1/).
Courses that run over several weeks are left out.
