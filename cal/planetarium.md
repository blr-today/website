> The Jawaharlal Nehru Planetarium in Bengaluru runs daily sky shows, talks and
  stargazing evenings on astronomy and science.

## Visiting the planetarium

The planetarium is on Sri T Choudaiah Road, near Ambedkar Veedhi in the city centre
(Sampangi Rama Nagar, Bengaluru 560001). Its 15 metre dome hosts sky-theatre shows
of about 40 minutes, in English and in Kannada.

- **Showtimes:** usually 12:30 pm and 4:30 pm on weekdays, with an extra 10:30 am show on weekends.
- **Tickets:** from ₹60 for children and ₹100 for adults, booked on BookMyShow.
- **Also on:** monthly stargazing, science club sessions and talks, listed here as they are announced.

Events on this page are read from the planetarium's Instagram posts and its
BookMyShow listings of the daily sky shows.
