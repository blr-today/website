> North Bangalore is everything past Mekhri Circle and Hebbal flyover,
from RT Nagar and Kalyan Nagar up to Yelahanka.

Events on this page are made up of:

1. Events with addresses mentioning Hebbal, Jakkur, RT Nagar, Kalyan Nagar, HRBR Layout, Hennur, Sahakar Nagar, Yelahanka, Thanisandra, Manyata, Kammanahalli, Banaswadi, Sanjay Nagar or Vidyaranyapura, and programmes at Science Gallery Bengaluru.
2. Events no other area claims, whose coordinates fall within 13.012–13.150°N, 77.565–77.665°E.
3. And not listed in the [Unwanted Events](/cal/unwanted/) calendar.
