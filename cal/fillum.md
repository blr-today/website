> [Bangalore Film Forum](https://fillum.in/user/52) is an arthouse film club
  showcasing contemporary, independent, foreign and classic films, usually
  followed by a conversation. Screenings are ticketed on
  [Fillum](https://fillum.in/), a platform for hosting film screenings
  anywhere from a living room to an auditorium.

Events on this page are the upcoming Bengaluru screenings listed on the
club's Fillum page.
