Subscribe to a calendar in your calendar app, or follow it on Bluesky or the fediverse.

Socials: Events are posted 2-7 days before they happen. On Mastodon, search for the handle shown when you hover a button. All event posts come from a single account, and the calendar specific accounts just repost it. So you can follow multiple accounts that you are interested in, and you won't see any duplicates in your feed. We suggest following the Curated and your neighbourhood calendars.
